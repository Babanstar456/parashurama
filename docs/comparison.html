Where PARASHURAMA fits

It does not replace live fire. It moves basic learning and repetition off the range, so range time is spent on final validation.

  1. LearnThe Academy teaches each clue and decision in about 10 minutes, on any laptop.PARASHURAMA
  2. RepeatUnlimited scored drills that target each soldier's weak conditions, at no extra cost.PARASHURAMA
  3. ProveThe readiness board shows who is ready; live drills on the range confirm it with real equipment.PARASHURAMA + live range
